<objective>
Fix the apparent "transparent header" bug where feed/page titles bleed through the NewZ Masthead on scroll.

Purpose: Masthead background is already opaque (`bg-surface` = solid #0A0A0A dark / #FFFFFF light). The visual bleed is a z-index tie: Masthead is `z-20` and SegmentCard's h2 is `relative z-20` with `dark:-mt-8 dark:sm:-mt-10` negative margin pulling it upward. Same z-index → DOM-order paint wins → h2 paints over Masthead. The negative margin on the h2 is intentional (load-bearing dark-mode video overlap effect) and must NOT be touched.

Output: Masthead's z-index bumped from `z-20` to `z-30` so global app chrome always paints above page content. Aligns Masthead with existing BottomTabBar convention (already `z-30`).
</objective>
